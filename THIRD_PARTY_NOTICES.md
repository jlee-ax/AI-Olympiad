## pstack

The repository-local KOAI skill procedures and workflow design substantially adapt pstack by Lauren Tan. Upstream: https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/c742dd61706fbacc1b95f1a20b7b988cf096ce6c/pstack (v0.15.36, 2026-10-27).

Adaptations retain orchestration, scoped recall, bounded hypothesis/measurement loops, independent review, decision trails, and correction/reflection principles. They add education-specific evidence distinctions and a local recorded-state planner. This project is not an official Cursor or pstack distribution, and the upstream project's capabilities or production claims do not establish this adaptation's efficacy.
