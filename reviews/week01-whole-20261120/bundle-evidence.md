
A `current` result requires the registered raw files, the dated checkpoint, and its evidence files to agree. The independent report must cover every member and return an overall pass and a pass per member. Each reviewed guide must retain its exact bytes. For notebooks, the reader and renderer hash every ordered cell after removing saved outputs, execution counts and execution timing metadata. They retain identifiers, text, attachments and authored metadata. The release identity additionally pins the complete published notebook and HTML bytes. The checker compares every published notebook and HTML fingerprint supplied by the reader. An early receipt still described the four handouts before full-size mobile figures were added. Substituting that genuine receipt in a copy flagged all four HTML files even after its evidence-file digest was updated. The reader then checked the final handouts and supplied replacement fingerprints.

A changed member or its assessment reopens the release. Inspect the reported paths, rerun the affected functional and delivery checks, and obtain a fresh independent decision when reviewed material changes. Preserve earlier review results in their dated directory. The existing four member identities remain protected. An authorized additional workbook needs a distinct in-week student notebook, guide and HTML handout, registered assessment source and data, and its own independent pass. The fixture-only extension in `test_bundle.py` exercises that path without adding a workbook to the course.

The checkpoint verifies consistency with recorded evidence. Repository review and the provenance of the independent reader establish who issued that evidence. The checker uses ordinary SHA-256 digests and cannot authenticate a person or prevent someone with write access from replacing both the material and its evidence. These checks concern the instructional material. Student learning, independence and observed time require student records.
