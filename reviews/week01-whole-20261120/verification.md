## Why a working release failed the instructional review

The registered Week 1 minimum explicitly called for distinguishing a row index from a persistent identifier and finding a gallery identifier from a row of scores. Its only transfer decision concerned moving the gallery columns. Authors could satisfy that requirement with a supplied score matrix and instructions for a lookup. The textbook used a pot-watering story as its introduction, asked students to “find a point's original row,” and even reversed the verbal subtraction in the definition of signed deviation. In a scatterplot, multiple different records can occupy the very same point. The distinction matters for classification and was precisely what the previous wording obscured. ## What the four books now teach

| Material | Instruction and independent application |
| --- | --- |
| Integrated | The public scikit-learn digits sample provides actual 8×8 feature maps and labels. Students distinguish irreversible 4×4 pixel counts from reversible flattening, calculate descriptive moments, classify and retrieve using small vectors, and reason about limitations of two selected coordinates. |
| Python and tables | Named features and source indices connect a pandas table to selected pixel counts. Independent work distinguishes row and column reductions, marginal counts and paired observations. Synthetic CSV work retains an explicitly stated file and join contract. |
| Vectors and matrices | A grayscale array gives numerical definitions of linear transformations, mean and signed contrast. Students distinguish elementwise multiplication from matrix multiplication, decide whether scale matters in retrieval and construct examples for which a sequence mean loses the order. |
| Classification | The pinned Antique training data distinguish the two labeled classes from unknown labels. A small worked calculation derives class means and squared-distance prediction, then separates the effects of changes to training data and held-out labels. File-order and ZIP mechanics are downstream. |

## Worked counterexamples and executable checks

- Original images 10 and 26 have different classes, 0 and 6. Both have (16,16) in regions (2,2) and (4,2). They differ at region (0,3), where the counts are 9 and 13. Selecting only the first two regions gives squared distance zero. In all 64 coordinates the squared distance is 1,756. Human interpretation and numerical outputs are both inspectable.
- On a copy of real measurements [0,10,16], masking the last value gives observed mean 5. Replacing the missing value with zero gives 10/3. The underlying unmasked mean is 26/3. In the model experiment, changing a held-out label can change accuracy while leaving both fitted centers and predictions intact.
- On query (1,1) and candidates (4,0), (1,1), the raw dot product chooses the former and cosine chooses the latter. Scaling a vector and permuting a candidate jointly with its column are different transformations with different consequences. The source exercise asks students to predict when the choice changes.
- The reference for indistinguishable labeled pairs is checked against an independently computed grouping by equal feature signatures for all 24 sample permutations and all eight feature subsets of a four-row matrix. Cases with no samples, one sample and no retained coordinates are included separately. A lookup returning candidate numbers cannot implement this function. [Transfer checks](../week01-main-iteration-20261120/check_transfer.py)
