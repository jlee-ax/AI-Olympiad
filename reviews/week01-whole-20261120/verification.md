A learner starts with the integrated sequence or uses the standalone foundation. A learner sent to a companion can identify the section to open, the decision to revisit and the point of return. Vector computation, model fitting and archive checks have conditional entry points. The weeks after Week 1 still own training design, model comparison, tensors and domain-specific tasks. The established allocation is 120 minutes in class with a 5–7 hour self-study planning range. No additional required book was warranted. Each sampled shortfall had an identifiable home in one of the existing four materials. Completing all four notebooks as assignments would double-count work.

## Basis for the selected depth

The domestic 2027 notice includes both written and coding responses from the announced scope. Its linked syllabus carries the IOAI 2026 heading and names Python, NumPy/pandas and Matplotlib/Seaborn. The 2026 public sample was prepared at high-school level for study and has no published official answers. We used it to inspect the style of a written decision. Its bivariate-plot item supports checking what a marginal view loses. The new paired records and their answer are local exercises. [KOAI 2027 middle-school scope](https://kitpa.org/koai/2027) · [Official linked syllabus](https://kitpa.org/koai/syllabus) · [Public sample notice](https://kitpa.org/files/36)

IOAI's 2025 Restroom problem distinguishes query and gallery identity. Antique supplies known and unknown training labels and separate evaluation inputs. In the 2026 Potato task there is no training split. Find the Order returns the chronological rank for each file ID. These are different contracts. Supplying a score matrix, a pair of small feature tables or a vector of token IDs isolates a Week 1 decision without asking a beginner to train an image or audio model. [IOAI Restroom](https://github.com/IOAI-official/IOAI-2025/blob/39558e9a639d170bd92de91958fcdc915f670463/Individual-Contest/Restroom/Restroom.ipynb) · [IOAI Antique](https://github.com/IOAI-official/IOAI-2025/blob/39558e9a639d170bd92de91958fcdc915f670463/Individual-Contest/Antique/Antique.ipynb) · [IOAI Potato](https://github.com/IOAI-official/IOAI-2026/blob/e1963801aa4cf33a5a03520b39fa6620a7894c54/Individual-Contest/3_Potato/statement.md) · [IOAI Find the Order](https://github.com/IOAI-official/IOAI-2026/blob/e1963801aa4cf33a5a03520b39fa6620a7894c54/Individual-Contest/1_Find_the_Order/statement.md)
