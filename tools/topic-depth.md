# Review the depth of a lesson

Run from a full Git checkout with Python 3.10 or later.

    python3 -B tools/topic_depth.py freeze --root .
    python3 -B tools/topic_depth.py inspect --root .
    python3 -B tools/topic_depth.py check --root .

The default checks every registered active student entry. The Week 1 registration links the new foundations notebook to its independent reviews. An unresolved judgment, an unfinished round, a changed teacher key/CSV, or an unreviewed direct edit gives the final command a nonzero exit. To review another lesson, specify `--case reviews/<case>`. Machine checks establish byte identity, declared teaching order, scope and planned time. The named reviewers judge what students have to understand and do. Their identities and educational conclusions are accountable declarations.

## Before editing

Write `contract.json` in the case directory. Declare `schema_version: 1`, the `lesson_path`, `baseline` (full Git commit and raw notebook SHA-256, or null for a proven absent notebook), `author`, `audience`, `class_minutes`, and `companion_paths`. Each `source_inputs` entry has a repository-relative `path`, raw `sha256` and original `locator`. Each topic has an `id`, observable `objective`, learner `entry`, required `minimum`, explicit `ceiling`, independent `assessment`, direct `prerequisites`, planned `minutes` for class/homework/optional, and zero-based `sources` indices.

An independent reviewer writes `freeze-review.json` with `stage: freeze`, the raw `contract_sha256`, `reviewer`, `verdict`, `finding`, and one judgment per topic covering `foundation`, `ceiling` and `workload`. Store other judgments of the same contract under `freeze-reviews/`. Preserve superseded pre-edit contracts and findings in `criteria-history/<version>/{contract.json,freeze-review.json}`. Commit the accepted version and its source inputs while the notebook remains at its baseline. The checker locates that ancestor in Git and rejects a first draft committed with or before the freeze. Squashing away the freeze commit removes that proof.

## Review a round

1. Create `rounds/0001/candidate.ipynb` and exact companion snapshots inside this or an earlier round. Save a `round.json` naming the author, contract SHA-256, candidate SHA-256, `previous` (null at first), and `prior_reviews` (empty at first). Each subsequent round binds the preceding raw round SHA-256 and every preceding review filename and raw hash.
2. In `companions` bind every registered live `path` to its archive `snapshot` and raw `sha256`. Map every student cell exactly once to a topic's `class`, `homework` or `optional` list, in notebook order. For each topic declare `support`, `assessment`, the first-use cell keyed by each prerequisite in `prerequisite_uses`, and planned `minutes` for each tier. Support and assessment cite the topic's own cells. A prerequisite must occur before its dependent first use in an available tier. The reviewer reads what those cells actually teach.
3. Each `changes` entry names the `topic`, proposed `action`, `reason` and `alternative`. Every reopened topic needs an explanation. Independent reviewers write their own JSON receipts under the round's `reviews/`. Bind `stage: candidate`, raw `contract_sha256`, `round_sha256`, `candidate_sha256` and `reviewer`. In `topics` give `verdict` (pass/revise/uncertain), actual `cells` and specific `foundation`, `ceiling`, `workload` and `finding` observations. A pass cites support and assessment. Missing votes stay pending and disagreement stays open. An optional separate original, `external_receipt: {path, sha256}`, goes in this round's `external/`. Its reviewer, subject and topic judgments must match exactly.
4. Run `inspect` to see changed topics, dependents and outstanding judgments. Editing a cell's content under its existing ID, changing its tier, changing prerequisite-use or time mappings, or changing a companion can trigger reinspection. Earlier passes carry over unchanged material. Unrelated insertions preserve independent topics below them. All companions conservatively trigger review in this case.
5. Copy the accepted notebook and companion bytes to their active paths and run `check`. Each previously committed student version must appear in the archive. A release check on an archived candidate verifies its ordinal as well as its bytes. Earlier ordinals retain their historical judgments even when two notebooks are byte-identical and the answer keys differ. Committed round files are append-only. File further judgments about the current subject before opening a subsequent round.

An adverse judgment can clear after a material change to its cited cells. A reviewer pointing to a data or key defect can declare `companion_spans: [{path, lines: [first, last]}]`. Each range must identify a unique, nonblank excerpt in that round's exact snapshot. Moving the same excerpt or changing an unrelated footnote cannot clear the finding. For deliberate no-change adjudication, a fresh independent reviewer attaches `counterevidence: {path, sha256}` to their topic judgment. Place the JSON under this round's `counterevidence/` and include `reviewer`, `round_sha256`, `contract_sha256`, `candidate_sha256`, `topic`, `cells`, `prior_reviews` and the concrete `finding`. Preserve the underlying independent work alongside it. Identical iterations do not count as progress.

The [Week 1 case](../reviews/week01-depth-20261118/) and [Git-backed test cases](../tests/test_topic_depth.py) show the fields.

## Revision and advancement

The quality evaluator returns a `topic_depth` report for registered subjects. Add its `manifest` to the workflow state's `context.inputs` alongside the discovery manifest and refresh the context fingerprint. This binds the selected baseline or archived ordinal, source basis, raw reviews and gate implementation. New judgments on the same notebook can change the permitted operation.

The planner emits `revise_topic_depth`, `review_topic_depth`, `resolve_topic_depth` or `adjudicate_topic_depth` for an open subject. A broken binding can request restoration of evidence. Existing agreements, budgets and stop conditions apply. For `revise_topic_depth`, `material_revision.py apply` takes the selected `work_key`, `author`, ordinary notebook `operations` and a typed `intent` with `actions` and a `routes` entry keyed by the selected topic ID. A patch can include additional currently adverse topics in `related_topics` when each has its own typed route. It checks the actual scoped diff. Use `reviews/<case>/rounds/NNNN/candidate.ipynb` as output. Verification and `advance_lesson` require the existing material checks and the independent depth verdict. Pending judgments and conflicts require review before a typed correction. Staged verification checks the archived snapshots. The final gate checks the published companion paths against those snapshots. The final CI job also executes the published notebook against its data in a fresh kernel.

## Refresh a source or criterion

Preserve the original contract, its source version and every previous round. Under `refreshes/0001/` add a new `contract.json` and `review.json`. This bounded procedure retains lesson identity, companion paths, original baseline, class budget, topic IDs and source paths. It permits revised topic criteria, prerequisite edges, allocations and updated source bytes. A different topic universe needs its own registration.

The review uses `stage: refresh`, `schema_version: 1`, `reviewer`, `finding`, `previous_contract_sha256`, `previous_review_sha256`, `contract_sha256`, `after_round`, `previous_round_sha256`, `prior_reviews` and `topics`. Bind the prior round and its receipts, or null/empty before the first round. Each topic gives a `decision` of `carry` or `reopen`, a substantive `finding` and `sources`. For every updated source used by the topic, key its zero-based index to the reviewed old/new line ranges. For example, `"sources": {"0": [{"old_lines": [31, 36], "new_lines": [34, 39]}]}`. Use an empty object for a topic without updated source inputs.

For `carry`, each cited excerpt must contain identical nonblank bytes in the old and new Git blobs, with unchanged topic criteria, audience and source locator. The independent reader decides whether the spans encompass the relevant requirement. A changed excerpt, prerequisite or criterion requires `reopen`. Dependencies reopen downstream topics. Commit the successor basis and updated sources before the next draft. A carry-only refresh can preserve the current material judgment. A reopened topic needs a new bound review and a rationale in the next round. Older rounds replay against their own basis and retain their original findings and hashes.
